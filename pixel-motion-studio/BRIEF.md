# Lark · Pixel Motion Studio

The viewer is for inspecting the feel and construction of a single original character. Open on Lark's idle cycle at 2×, with all four actions within reach. The character is a fantasy mail courier whose teal hood, orange scarf, satchel and envelope should read through all the action poses. Switching to Jump or Attack should make the anticipation, action and recovery legible in order. Step through contact poses with the guide on, or compare two actions on the same clock and check where the shorter clip settles.

- [ ] Original courier with four distinct, coherent motions and stable canvas framing.
- [ ] Transparent PNG sheets and frames, with editable pose and timing source.
- [ ] All transport, stage and comparison controls usable on desktop and narrow screens.
- [ ] Offline HTML opens from disk, including with browser networking disabled.
- [ ] Inspect every clip and both viewport sizes. Record automated results separately from visual judgments.
